Edit the steps

Click a cell to place a note on a step, click again to remove it. Accent: a stronger step. Tie: the note glides into the next step without being retriggered. Vel and Gate: drag a bar up or down, or across several steps to draw them all at once. Higher means louder or longer. Arrow keys also work.

About Bass Assembly

Bass Assembly helps when you stare at an empty sequencer. Pick a style, a root note and a scale, press generate, and you get a short bass pattern of 8 or 16 steps with its own accents, ties, velocity and gate. Every result is editable, so a happy accident becomes a starting point.

The generator follows the habits of each genre. Acid favors minor pentatonic notes, accents and slides. EBM keeps a tight, driving pulse on the beat. Industrial leaves more space and leans on the root. Dark wave moves between the root, the fifth and the minor third.

What you can do

  • Generate, randomize or gently vary a bassline, and keep the ideas you like.
  • Listen in the browser with an adjustable synth: wave, filter, drive, sub, glide, delay and reverb.
  • Add swing and humanize for a less rigid feel.
  • Export a MIDI file, or copy the notes to reproduce them on your own gear.

FAQ

What is Bass Assembly?

Bass Assembly is a free bassline generator and 16 step sequencer that runs in your browser. It suggests short bass patterns in styles such as acid, EBM, industrial and dark wave, plays them with a built-in synth, and lets you edit every step by hand.

How do I make an acid bassline?

Choose the Acid style, keep the minor pentatonic scale and raise the density a little. Use ties for slides, add accents on a few steps, and push the resonance in the Sound panel. Generate again until a pattern grooves, then fine tune notes, velocity and gate in the grid.

Can I export the bassline as a MIDI file?

Yes. Export 1, 2 or 4 bars as a .mid file and drag it into your DAW, or load it into any hardware or software sequencer that reads MIDI files. Swing and humanize are included in the export.

How do I play the pattern on my own synth or pad controller?

The Play it on your gear panel shows the note of every step on a piano keyboard and on a generic two row pad layout. The Copy on your instrument panel lists the note, MIDI number, velocity and gate of each step, and you can copy it as text.

What does Humanize do?

Humanize adds small random changes to timing, velocity and note length, so the pattern feels less mechanical. Keep it at 0% for strict styles such as EBM or acid, and raise it a little for a looser, more played feel.

Is Bass Assembly free and open source?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and everything runs locally in your browser: your ideas stay on your device. The code is released under the MIT license and you can download it on GitHub.

Does Bass Assembly track me?

No. There are no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no trackers, and the page loads nothing from other sites: even the fonts are hosted here. The basslines, the sound and the MIDI files are made in your browser, nothing is sent to a server. Your theme and saved ideas are kept in your browser storage and never leave your device. Like any website, the web host may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security. As the code is open source, you can also download it from GitHub and run Bass Assembly on your own computer.
